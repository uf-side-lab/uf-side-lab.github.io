Outlook

Future directions

01

Predictive device design from GaN physics

We aim to establish theoretical frameworks for predicting transistor characteristics such as threshold voltage.

02

Logic devices based on GaN heterostructures

We aim to realize p-type transistors and extend the technology toward complementary logic devices.

03

From Ga₂O₃ crystals to microwave devices

Starting with electrical characterization, we advance technology toward microwave-device applications.

04

Ultra-high-frequency applications of InP heterostructures

We envision millimeter-wave and terahertz amplifiers and low-noise amplifiers for cryogenic environments.
