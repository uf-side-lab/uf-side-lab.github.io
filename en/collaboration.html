Outlook

Future Directions

01

Predicting Device Characteristics from GaN Physics

Based on the physics of polarization in GaN-based semiconductors, we aim to establish theoretical frameworks for predicting transistor characteristics such as threshold voltage.

02

Logic Devices Based on GaN Heterostructures

We aim to realize p-type transistors using GaN-based semiconductors and to extend this technology toward complementary logic devices.

03

From Ga₂O₃ Crystals to Microwave Devices

Starting with the electrical characterization of Ga₂O₃ crystals, we seek to advance the technology toward microwave-device applications.

04

Ultra-High-Frequency Applications of InP Heterostructures

We envision applications such as mmWave and THz amplifiers, as well as low-noise amplifiers operating at cryogenic temperatures.
