01 / GaN HEMT

GaN High electron Mobility Transistors (HEMTs)

We pursue high-performance devices and integrated circuits for high-frequency and high-voltage applications by utilizing gallium-nitride (GaN)-based heterostructures with two-dimensional electron systems.

02 / InP HEMT

Ultra-High-Frequency and Low-Noise-Devices

We explore InP-based devices for applications in the millimeter-wave (mm Wave) and terahertz (THz) frequency ranges, as well as in cryogenic environments.

03 / Ga₂O₃

Electrical Characterization of Gallium Oxide

From material characterization to device development, we aim to advance Ga₂O₃ technology toward microwave and power-device applications.
